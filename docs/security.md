# Security Policy

## Our Commitment to Security

We take the security of our project and the safety of our users' data very seriously. As part of our commitment to ensuring a secure environment, we follow a "Shift Left" security methodology, emphasizing the integration of security measures early in the software development lifecycle.

## Sensitive Information

It is strictly prohibited to commit Personally Identifiable Information (PII), Protected Health Information (PHI), HIPAA-related data, or any other forms of sensitive information to this repository. Always review your commits to ensure that sensitive information is not accidentally included.

## Using Snyk for Enhanced Security

We use Snyk as our primary tool for identifying and resolving security vulnerabilities. Snyk helps us to integrate security into our development process from the start, ensuring that our codebase remains secure against known vulnerabilities.

### IDE Integration

We strongly encourage all contributors to integrate Snyk into their Integrated Development Environment (IDE). This integration provides real-time feedback and alerts about potential vulnerabilities as you code, helping to prevent the introduction of security issues.

For information on integrating Snyk with your IDE, please refer to the [Snyk documentation](https://docs.snyk.io/integrate-with-snyk/ide-tools).

## Reporting Security Issues

If you discover a security issue within the project, or if you believe you have found sensitive information committed to the repository, please refrain from public disclosure and follow our reporting process outlined below.

### Contacting the Cybersecurity Application Team

Please escalate any security concerns or incidents directly to our Cybersecurity Data Team by sending an email to the [Cybersecurity Application Security team](mailto:appsec@imail.org). Your report will be reviewed and handled with the utmost discretion.

When reporting a security issue, please include:
- A clear description of the issue
- Steps to reproduce the issue (if applicable)
- Any relevant screenshots or output

We appreciate your help in maintaining the security and privacy of our project. All reports are taken seriously, and we are committed to investigating and resolving security issues in a timely manner.

## Thank You

Your contributions and vigilance help keep our project and its users safe. We deeply appreciate your efforts to adhere to our security policies and your proactive approach to identifying and addressing security issues.
